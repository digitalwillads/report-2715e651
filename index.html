What this means

Three habits

  1. 1
    Answer every reply.We never answered 741 leads who wrote to us.
  2. 2
    Follow up at least twice when a lead goes quiet.The first follow-up gets an answer 38% of the time, the second 21%. Today 72% of quiet leads get no follow-up.
  3. 3
    Never let a booked call end in silence.66% of booked calls end with the lead going quiet. After each call, send a short recap and the next step.
